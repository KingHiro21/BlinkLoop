Cookie Policy

Effective September 7, 2026 · BlinkLoop, Mandaue City, Cebu, Philippines

This page lists exactly what blinkloop-ph.com stores in your browser and which other companies see your requests. We keep it short because there is not much: no advertising cookies, no social media pixels, and no tracking unless you say yes.

In one line. The public website stores your light or dark theme choice and, if you make one, your analytics choice. That is all, unless you are a BlinkLoop staff member signing in. Analytics only runs after you accept it in the banner.

1. Cookies and local storage

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to keep. Local storage is a similar space in your browser that only the website that wrote it can read. Both stay on your device until they expire or you delete them.

Visitors never receive these. They exist only on devices used by the BlinkLoop team.

Optional: analytics (only with your consent)

We may use Google Analytics 4 to learn which pages help visitors and which do not. It is switched off until two things happen: we enable it on our side, and you choose "Accept analytics" in the banner. If both happen, Google sets cookies named _ga and _ga_<id> (they last up to 2 years) and receives your page views with a shortened IP address. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, which appears whenever analytics is enabled.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as "Essential only" and never show the banner.

3. Other companies that may see your requests

  • Vercel hosts this website. Like every host, it records technical logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) to run and protect the service. Fonts, images and pages are all served from our own domain.
  • Google receives what you type in the contact form, because the form is delivered to our info@blinkloopph.com mailbox through a Google Apps Script. Google also receives analytics data, but only after you consent.
  • Supabase stores the BlinkLoop team chat. Visitors never send anything to it.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Messenger, Viber, Google Maps and client websites only open when you click them, and take you to those services under their own policies.

We do not embed videos, social feeds, advertising networks or chat widgets from other companies.

4. Managing cookies

  • Use the "Cookie settings" link in the footer to change your analytics choice (shown when analytics is enabled).
  • Every browser lets you view, block and delete cookies and local storage in its settings. Blocking essential storage only means the site forgets your theme choice.
  • Staff can sign out to remove their session cookies.

5. Changes

If we add a tool that stores something new in your browser, we update this page and, where consent is needed, the banner asks you again.

6. Contact

Questions: info@blinkloopph.com. How we handle personal information more broadly is in our Privacy Policy.
