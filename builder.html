    How this works

    Four things and you know the whole tool.

    1
    Add blocks

    Click any block in the left panel, drag it onto the page, or use the ＋ buttons that appear on the page itself.

    2
    Click text to edit it

    Headings, paragraphs, buttons: click them right on the page and type. Lists like pricing plans are edited in the right panel.

    3
    Make it yours in Design

    The Design tab (top right) changes colors, fonts, and corner roundness for the whole site at once.

    4
    Export when ready

    Export gives you the finished website as one clean HTML file. Exporting is part of client access. On a trial you can build, preview, and save your draft, and we take it from there.

    Handy keys: Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Delete removes the selected block · Esc deselects.

    Add a block

    It will be inserted right where you clicked ＋.

    Export your site

    You get one self-contained HTML file, and that file is the website. No plugins, nothing to install.

    Publish with BlinkLoop

    One click puts this site online at your-name.blinkloop-ph.com. Its contact form delivers to the Leads list in Admin.

    To put it online: name the file index.html and drop it into your hosting. For Vercel, add it to your repo and push, or drag the file into a new Vercel project. It's live in seconds. Want BlinkLoop to host and maintain it for you? That's exactly what our care plans are for.

    Save or load your project

    Your work autosaves in this browser automatically. Imported pages are the exception: they stay in memory until you save them. A project file is for moving between devices, keeping a backup, or sending your draft to us.
