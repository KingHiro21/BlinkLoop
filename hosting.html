Two different jobs

Hosting keeps it online. Maintenance keeps it healthy.

They sound similar, but they solve different problems. Here is exactly what each one covers.

Hosting

Keeping your website online

The infrastructure work that keeps your site reachable, fast, and safe, every day of the year.

  • Servers & deployments
  • SSL certificates
  • DNS management
  • Automated backups
  • Uptime monitoring
  • Technical configuration
Maintenance

Keeping your website updated

The hands-on care that keeps your content current and your site in good shape as your business changes.

  • Content & image updates
  • Security updates
  • Performance optimization
  • Minor website changes
  • Troubleshooting
  • Technical updates

Maintenance is included in Business and Premium hosting plans. Each plan covers a defined scope of updates per year, not unlimited changes.
